Swami Samarth - An Introduction

The birth details of Swami Samarth, considered the third avatar of Sri Dattatreya, remain highly secretive. Sri Dattatreya’s first avatar, Sripada Srivallabha (1320 AD), predicted the future incarnations.

Spiritual Lineage

The second avatar appeared as Sri Narasimha Saraswati (1450 AD). After concluding that avatar, he performed penance in the Kadali Vana for 300 years before emerging as Swami Samarth in Maharashtra.

Akkalkot Maharaj

Swami Samarth stayed in Akkalkot for twenty years and attained Samadhi in a devotee’s house there, earning the name “Akkalkot Maharaj.”

Divine presence, compassionate grace, and protection define the Swami Samarth path.

Origin in Kanchipuram

The desire to build a temple for Paramaguru Sri Sainath Sadguru in Marudham (Maruthipuram) took root in 2011.

2015

After chanting the Hanuman Chalisa 108 times over 18 days at the local Maruti temple, a worship center was inaugurated with Padukas and portrait of Sai Nath.

2017

The thought of building a dedicated Paduka Mandir took deep root.

2021

With the blessings of the Gurus, Bhoomi Puja was performed on February 24.

Guided by Devotion

Each milestone strengthened the resolve to create a sacred space where devotees can experience darshan, seva, and spiritual upliftment.

Viswaroopa Darshan & Structure

The temple consists of three levels: Underground (Thazhthalam), Sanctum Sanctorum (Garbhagriha), and the Tower (Gopuram).

Underground

Maruthipuram Swami Samarth Maharaj Viswaroopa Darshan represents Swami’s holy feet up to the waist.

Garbhagriha

Sri Nrusimha Saraswati, Sri Swami Samarth, Sri Sai Nath, Audumbar Moola Paduka, and Panchaloha Idol of Dattatreya are consecrated over sacred Yantras.

Sacred Yantras include Sri Chakra, Vaibhava Lakshmi, Hanuman, and Datta Yantras.

Goshala - The Living Form of Dharma

In Hindu Dharma, the cow is Gomatha, a divine mother who nurtures life. Supporting the Goshala is a service that sustains harmony with nature.

Why Goshala Matters

Kamadhenu is praised as the divine cow who bestows prosperity. Lord Krishna, as Gopala, lived with cows and taught Ahimsa and harmony with nature.

Benefits of Supporting

  • Merit of Go-Dhanam
  • Family harmony
  • Protection of nature
  • Traditional agriculture
Lend a hand - Save a life. Service to Goshala is service to God.
